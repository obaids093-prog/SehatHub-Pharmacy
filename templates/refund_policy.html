Refund Policy

Return Eligibility

You can request a return within 2 days of receiving your order under the following conditions:

  • check_circle Product is unopened, unused, and in its original sealed packaging
  • check_circle Product has a manufacturing defect (photo/video evidence required)
  • check_circle Wrong product was shipped by us

Non-Returnable Items

  • cancel Cold-chain items requiring refrigeration (e.g. insulin, vaccines)
  • cancel Any medicine that has been opened, unsealed, or partially used, for safety reasons
  • cancel Prescription medicines once dispensed against a verified prescription

How to Request a Return

  1. Contact us via phone or email within 2 days of delivery
  2. Provide your order number and the reason for return
  3. We will review and approve or reject your request within 1-2 business days
  4. If approved, we'll send instructions for returning the item

Return Shipping Cost

  • local_shipping If the return is due to our error (defect or wrong product): we cover return shipping
  • local_shipping If the return is due to a change of mind: the customer covers return shipping

Refund Method

Once we receive and inspect the returned product:

  • payments Refund is processed within 5-7 business days
  • payments Since orders are Cash on Delivery, refunds are sent via bank transfer or JazzCash/EasyPaisa to the account you provide
  • payments You'll receive confirmation once the refund has been sent
