About SehatHub

Reliable healthcare, delivered with care.

Our Story

SehatHub started with a simple idea: getting medicines shouldn't mean standing in line at a crowded pharmacy, especially when you're unwell or caring for someone who is. We built SehatHub to bring genuine, licensed medicines and healthcare essentials to your doorstep across Pakistan - quickly, safely, and with the same care a trusted neighborhood pharmacist would give you.

Our Mission

To make essential healthcare accessible to every household in Pakistan by combining a wide catalog of genuine medicines with fast, dependable delivery - all backed by licensed pharmacists who verify every prescription before it's dispensed.
