How We Use It

  • check_circle To process and deliver your orders

Who Can See Your Prescription

Prescription images are only visible to our licensed pharmacist staff for verification purposes, and to you in your own account. They are not shared publicly or with any party outside the order fulfillment process.

Data Security

Your password is never stored in plain text - it's protected using industry-standard hashing. Account access is protected against common attacks, and sensitive actions on your account require you to be logged in.
